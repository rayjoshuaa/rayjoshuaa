## 👋 About Me

I'm an **ICT, Computer Science & Robotics teacher** in Bangkok with **6+ years in IT** and **3+ years in academe** — and I build software on the side that solves real problems for schools and small businesses.

- 🏫 Teaching Kindergarten to Secondary: **Scratch & Python** for young learners, **LEGO & Arduino robotics**, and C++, C#, Java, Python & PHP at higher levels
- 🏢 Former **Business Systems Analyst** — process flows, BRDs, and QA/UAT/go-live for **10+ enterprise systems** (HRIS, DMS, ERP)
- 💻 Freelance & remote services: **CMS websites** (WordPress / Elementor / Shopify), **social media management**, and **custom web development**
- 🤖 I build with AI-assisted workflows daily — and I teach students to use technology responsibly
- 🗣️ Languages: English · Tagalog · Bisaya (Cebuano)
- 🤝 **Open to collaborations on any project related to education**
- 🌱 Currently: teaching ICT & Robotics at SIAMIS in Bangkok, and sharing daily notes on AI, ICT and robotics in education
- 🎓 Verified GitHub Education teacher — now building with Copilot Pro
- 🐱 Quality control: my orange cat supervises every build
